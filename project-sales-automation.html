Executive Summary

From manual feed-scanning to a governed revenue operating system

The client's outbound motion depended entirely on SDRs manually scanning public channels for buying signals — a process that was slow, inconsistent, and impossible to attribute. I led the architecture, build, and governance program for an AI revenue system that codified that manual judgment into a repeatable pipeline: one that surfaces genuine intent, enriches it, and hands reps a CRM-ready, human-approved opportunity instead of a cold guess.

The Business Problem

The client's growth plan called for a 40% increase in outbound-sourced pipeline, but the existing process could not scale. SDRs spent an estimated 60% of their working day manually scanning social and community channels for buying signals, then researching and drafting outreach one lead at a time. Capture was inconsistent across reps, attribution back to specific signals was effectively impossible, and leadership had no reliable way to measure which channels or messages were actually driving revenue.

A baseline audit at engagement kickoff quantified the gap: average prospecting time of 4.2 hours per qualified opportunity, an outreach approval bottleneck that stalled high-intent leads for 24–48 hours, and a reported pipeline-to-close conversion rate roughly 35% below the team's target. The revenue leadership team needed a system that could scale lead capture without surrendering control of who gets contacted, when, and how — a governance requirement, not just a productivity one.

The Solution

I designed and delivered a closed-loop, governance-first revenue operating system that monitors targeted public channels for buying-intent signals, classifies genuine intent using a tuned AI model, enriches qualifying accounts and contacts, and writes clean, deduplicated records directly into the CRM. Every high-intent match generates a context-rich, editable outreach draft that is routed to the assigned rep in Slack for explicit human approval — nothing is sent without a person in the loop.

This governance posture — human approval before every send, full attribution back to the originating signal, and a standardized qualification threshold applied consistently across the team — was the deciding factor in getting sales leadership and compliance to sign off on an AI-driven outbound motion.

  • Ingestion pipeline with scoped queries and batching, engineered for signal quality and cost discipline at production volume.
